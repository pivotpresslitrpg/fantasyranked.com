LitRPG is a genre defined by game-system mechanics embedded directly into narrative fiction. It is characterized by stat screens, leveling progressions, and skill acquisition that drive both character development and plot — distinct from general fantasy in that the rules of the world are explicit, numerical, and load-bearing.

Finding your next read in this space used to mean digging through Reddit threads, Discord servers, and Amazon also-boughts. [LitRPGTools.com](https://litrpgtools.com) changes that equation.
