Xianxia cultivation fantasy is a subgenre built on the pursuit of power through disciplined practice, spiritual refinement, and the ruthless hierarchy of realms. It is characterized by stage-gated progression, intense cultivation sessions, and a world where the gap between the weak and the powerful is vast enough to feel cosmological.

David North's *River of Fate* sits right in that tradition — a cultivation story with genuine stakes, a protagonist who earns every breakthrough, and a world that rewards readers who love watching a character go from nothing to something extraordinary. If you've finished it and you're already hungry for what's next, here's where to look.

## What to Read After River of Fate

Based on our analysis of 50,000+ titles across the LitRPG and progression fantasy landscape, the books below score highest among readers who rate *River of Fate* favorably. According to community data from [LitRPGTools.com](https://litrpgtools.com), cultivation-adjacent titles with hard magic systems and measurable progression see 34% higher completion rates than the genre average — which tracks exactly with what makes *River of Fate* so readable.

**Ranked by thematic overlap and community rating:**

1. **Cradle series by Will Wight** — If *River of Fate* gave you the cultivation itch, Wight's *Unsouled* is the modern benchmark for Western xianxia. The Sacred Valley to Cradle pipeline is one of the most satisfying power climbs in all of progression fantasy, and Lindon's early-realm struggles will feel very familiar.

2. **The Primal Hunter by Zogarth** — Cultivation logic meets system apocalypse in this monster of a series. Zogarth builds a progression ladder that's just as rigorous as anything in xianxia, but wraps it in a blue-box LitRPG shell that makes the stat tracking irresistible.

3. **Defiance of the Fall by J.F. Brink** — One of the heaviest-hitting system apocalypse series in the space, *Defiance* scratches the same itch as *River of Fate* through sheer scale of progression and a protagonist who fights for every level. Check out our [top power fantasy books](/rankings/top-power-fantasy-books/) list — this series ranks near the top for a reason.

4. **Beneath the Dragoneye Moons by Selkie Myth** — A progression fantasy that takes the long view on power growth. The character work is exceptional, the world-building layered, and the class/skill system rewards the kind of reader who loves understanding how a magic system actually functions. Currently holding a 5.0★ rating in our database — one of the highest in the genre.

5. **Wild Era by David North** — If you loved *River of Fate*, North's *Wild Era* is the obvious next stop from the same author. It's LitRPG progression with a distinct evolutionary flavor, and it carries that same sense of a world that operates by real internal logic.

6. **He Who Fights With Monsters by Shirtaloon** — Jason Asano's journey is a masterclass in slow-burn power escalation wrapped in a genuinely funny protagonist voice. The cultivation-adjacent progression and deep skill tree will feel right at home for *River of Fate* readers.

7. **Overpowered Wizard by Hunter Mythos** — Currently a 5.0★ title in our rankings, this one delivers exactly what the title promises: a magic system with genuine mechanical depth and a protagonist who scales hard. According to reader ratings on LitRPGTools.com, it over-indexes with readers who love both xianxia and Western LitRPG crossovers.
