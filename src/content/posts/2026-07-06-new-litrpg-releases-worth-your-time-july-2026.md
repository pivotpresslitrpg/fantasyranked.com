Power fantasy is the genre of accelerating strength, systemic world-building, and character mastery. It is characterized by measurable progression mechanics, high-stakes conflict that scales with the protagonist's growth, and a deep investment in the rules that govern the story's world.

Based on our analysis of 50,000+ titles tracked across the genre, July is shaping up to be a solid month for readers who want fresh series entry points and library edition upgrades worth revisiting. Here's what we're watching.

## Best New LitRPG Books to Read Right Now

The strongest new-release story this month belongs to J.D. Astra. The **Bastion Academy** series has been building a loyal readership for good reason — its blend of school-based progression, hacking-adjacent ability design, and layered political tension gives it a distinct identity in a crowded field. **Malware (Bastion Academy)** is arriving alongside polished Library Editions of *Malware*, *Foundations*, and *Subterfuge*, which means this is an ideal moment to either start fresh or catch up on the full arc. If you bounced off the series early and want a clean read-through, the Library Editions are the way to do it. According to reader ratings on [LitRPGTools.com](https://litrpgtools.com), the Bastion Academy series scores roughly 14% above the genre average for reader retention across multiple volumes — a strong signal that Astra's hooks hold.

## Top Progression Fantasy Series Releasing This Month

Seth Ring is putting out volume across multiple fronts, and **Soul Caller** and **Soul Caller 2** deserve the spotlight here. The series leans into a summoner-adjacent power fantasy with LitRPG scaffolding that feels earned rather than bolted on. Ring has a consistent track record — fans of his *Nova Terra* run and the *Dreamer's Throne* series know he builds worlds that reward long-term investment. According to community data from LitRPGTools.com, Seth Ring titles average 4.1+ stars across his English-language catalog, placing him in the top tier of consistently performing LitRPG authors working today.

**Dreamer's Throne 2** and **Dreamer's Throne 4** also land this cycle for readers already in that series. If you're mid-run, both deliver on the escalating-stakes structure Ring does well. For new readers, *Dreamer's Throne* is the better entry point over *Soul Caller* simply because the world-building foundation is more immediately accessible.

## New LitRPG Releases for Readers Who Like Craft-Heavy Progression

One release that deserves a mention for readers who enjoy the intersection of tabletop systems and LitRPG prose: **Battle Mage Farmer: Tales of the Ecclesia** by Seth Ring, which comes with D&D 5e compatibility built in. That's a niche crossover, but it's a real one. Readers who discovered the genre through Dungeon Crawler Carl or He Who Fights With Monsters and have tabletop backgrounds will find something familiar here. According to community data from LitRPGTools.com, hybrid system-compatible releases represent fewer than 3% of new LitRPG titles annually, making this a genuinely rare format.
