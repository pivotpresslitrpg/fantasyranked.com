April 2026 is a dense month for progression fantasy. Three new Aethon series launched on April 7, and RinoZ drops Book of the Dead 5 on April 22 — a continuation that will generate more signal movement than most new series launches combined.

Here's how the April landscape looks based on current tracking data from LitRPGTools.com and Harem-Lit.com.

## The Big Continuation: Book of the Dead 5 (April 22)

Before covering new series, the most significant April release is a continuation.

**Book of the Dead by RinoZ** — five books deep on April 22 — is tracking as the second-highest anticipation signal in the current catalog behind only the DCC Book 8 build-up. RinoZ built the series on the back of Chrysalis's established community: readers who trusted his output after 11 Chrysalis volumes showed up for Book of the Dead from day one.

What the tracking data shows for Book of the Dead 5:

- Back-catalog acceleration is active. New readers discovering the series are progressing through Books 1-4 in compressed windows ahead of the release.
- Community rating consistency across Books 1-4 is strong. Unlike many series that show score decay after Book 2-3, Book of the Dead has maintained its rating floor through the mid-series run.
- Cross-platform tracking is solid. The series appears in both LitRPGTools.com's progression fantasy catalog and on Harem-Lit.com, reflecting that necromancer/outcast cultivation appeal crosses audience demographics.

**Entry ranking signal**: Established. High confidence. RinoZ delivering Book 5 of a well-rated series with active readership is a low-variance event.

## New Series Rankings: April 7 Launches

### Andy in the Apocalypse — Plum Parrot

**Signal confidence**: High, for Plum Parrot specifically. Lower for this specific title until data accumulates.

Plum Parrot is one of the most-tracked active authors on LitRPGTools.com. Victor of Tucson is a perennial top-10 tracker on the platform — the LitRPGTools.com community knows the author well.

Andy in the Apocalypse is described as an apocalyptic LitRPG centered on found family, courage under pressure, and "carving hope out of chaos." That framing maps directly to Victor of Tucson's emotional core — Plum Parrot's signature is warm, earned relationships in high-stakes settings. If that formula translates to an apocalyptic setting (and there's no reason to expect it wouldn't), the readership will follow.

What we're tracking: Day-1 reader acquisition rate relative to Victor of Tucson Book 1's initial discovery period. Cross-catalog readers who finish Victor of Tucson and immediately add Andy in the Apocalypse are the highest-quality signal for long-term series health.

**Fantasy Ranked entry position**: Watch list — top tracking priority for April new series.

### Rite of Ascension — Marc Mulero

**Signal confidence**: Low (new author, insufficient catalog data).

The premise combines elements with strong genre track records: weak-to-strong protagonist, dragon companion, magic academy arc, arena battles, slow-build progression. These are not novel elements — they describe a significant portion of successful progression fantasy — but the combination in a new series is a reasonable starting hypothesis.

Marc Mulero doesn't have a prior catalog on LitRPGTools.com or Harem-Lit.com, which means the initial signal will come entirely from first-read community ratings rather than author reputation transfer. That's a slower burn than established authors but makes the eventual data more clean — no prior-series halo effect.

**Fantasy Ranked entry position**: Monitoring. Flag for re-ranking at 60-day read-through data.

### Labyrinth of the Mad God — Magnus Grey

**Signal confidence**: Low (new author, limited data).

Described as "gritty, slowburn, apocalyptic LitRPG with a multi-dimensional twist." The gritty/slowburn framing positions it against the more kinetic mainstream of Aethon's catalog — closer to the darker end of the apocalyptic LitRPG spectrum. That audience exists and is loyal, but it's smaller and harder to acquire on first contact.

Cross-referencing LitRPGTools.com and Harem-Lit.com for early community engagement will determine whether the multi-dimensional premise creates differentiation or confusion.

**Fantasy Ranked entry position**: Monitoring. Lower priority than the Plum Parrot launch.
