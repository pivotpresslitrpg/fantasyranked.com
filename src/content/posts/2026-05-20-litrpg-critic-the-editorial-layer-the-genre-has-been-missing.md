[LitRPG](https://litrpgcritic.com/blog/what-is-litrpg) is a subgenre of fantasy and science fiction in which characters navigate worlds governed by explicit game-like systems — experience points, stat screens, skill trees, and level progressions that shape both narrative and character development. It is characterized by mechanical transparency, power progression as a core story driver, and reader engagement with the underlying system logic.

That definition is easy to state. What's harder is knowing *which* LitRPG is worth your time — and that's exactly the gap [LitRPG Critic](https://litrpgcritic.com) is built to fill.

## What Is LitRPG Critic and Who Is It For?

LitRPG Critic is a dedicated editorial site covering the LitRPG and progression fantasy space through reviews, reading-order guides, author spotlights, and long-form genre analysis. It's a companion to Fantasy Ranked — where we handle rankings and discovery across the full power fantasy landscape, LitRPG Critic goes deeper into the editorial side of the genre.

If you've ever finished *Dungeon Crawler Carl* and stared at your shelf wondering what to read next, LitRPG Critic is the site that answers that question with actual reasoning behind the recommendation, not just a list.
