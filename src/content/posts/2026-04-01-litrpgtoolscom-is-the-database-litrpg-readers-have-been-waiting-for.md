[LitRPG](https://litrpgcritic.com/blog/what-is-litrpg) is a genre defined by game-like systems embedded in narrative fiction — characters gain levels, assign stats, unlock skills, and navigate worlds governed by explicit rules. It is characterized by mechanical progression, quantified power growth, and reader engagement with the underlying systems as a core part of the story experience.

That last point matters. LitRPG readers don't just follow a plot — they engage with the mechanics. They argue skill build choices. They rank dungeon arcs. They theorize about system logic. And for a long time, the tooling to support that kind of engagement was scattered across Reddit threads and Discord servers with no central home.

[LitRPGTools.com](https://litrpgtools.com) is building that home.

## What Is LitRPGTools.com?

LitRPGTools.com is a dedicated LitRPG book database and community platform combining searchable title discovery, AI-powered generators, and a community ratings and reviews system. Based on our analysis of the platform's current feature set, it's one of the few tools built specifically around how LitRPG readers actually interact with the genre — not just cataloguing books, but engaging with the mechanics inside them.

The database is the foundation. Readers can search and browse titles across the genre, filter by system type, progression style, and sub-genre, and find community ratings that reflect what serious LitRPG readers actually think.
