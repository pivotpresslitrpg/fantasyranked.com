## What Can Readers Actually Do on the Platform?

The database is the foundation — you can search by subgenre, filter by community rating, and find titles with enough reader context to make a confident pick. If you've already worked through Dungeon Crawler Carl, He Who Fights With Monsters, or Dakota Krout's Completionist Chronicles and you're not sure what comes next, that search function is genuinely useful.

The more distinctive feature is the AI generator suite. Readers and writers can generate character builds, design skill trees, run dungeon encounters, create boss fights, and build out full world systems. These aren't gimmicks — they're the kind of tools that reflect a deep understanding of how LitRPG mechanics actually work on the page. The gamification layer ties it together. The platform rewards engagement — reading, reviewing, generating — which creates a feedback loop that improves the community data over time.
