[Harem fantasy](https://haremlitguide.com/blog/what-is-harem-fantasy) is a subgenre of men's romantic fantasy in which a male protagonist builds meaningful relationships with multiple female characters across an adventure or power-progression narrative. It is characterized by romantic tension woven into larger fantasy stakes, power scaling tied to relationships or bonds, and a tone that ranges from comedic to deeply dramatic depending on the series.

If you read across the full spectrum — [LitRPG](https://litrpgcritic.com/blog/what-is-litrpg), progression fiction, dark fantasy, cultivation — you already know that harem fantasy sits in a complicated spot. It has a massive, loyal readership and genuinely excellent titles, but general genre databases treat it as an afterthought. Harem-Lit.com exists specifically to fix that.

Allure is the platform's gacha card collector game, built around character cards drawn from harem novels. Daily pulls, rarity tiers, and card battles make it a functional light game rather than a cosmetic feature. According to community data from Harem-Lit.com, Allure has become one of the more active engagement features on the platform, with players pulling cards tied to characters from popular series across the catalog.

It's a smart design decision. Readers already have strong attachments to specific characters — turning that into a collectible format creates a reason to stay engaged between releases.
